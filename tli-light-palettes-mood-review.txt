MOOD REVIEW · TLI Light Palettes
Mood: civic, institutional, serious and trustworthy, rooted in Federal navy and the materials of public buildings (limestone, marble, granite, bronze, parchment, official seals): muted, dusty and greyed tones with navy and logo-red anchors; never pastel, candy, blush, lavender or playful
Ask of every theme: does this color fit the mood, the palette's brief and the theme's job? Record misfits in settings mood_review.
